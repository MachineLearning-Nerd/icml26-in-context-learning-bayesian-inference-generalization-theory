# Method

The verifier reconstructs likelihood-ratio chaining, conditional-MGF
concentration, posterior odds, total variance over the task index, and the
Bayes-to-minimax comparison. It uses
`lambda=D/[2(nu^2+bD/2)]`; exact algebra verifies both its MGF-domain
admissibility and the displayed rate. Four rational checkers are independent
bug detectors, while the symbolic derivation supplies the universal quantifier.
