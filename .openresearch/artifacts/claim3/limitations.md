# Limitations and deviations

This is a proof reconstruction, not an empirical measurement of how tightly the
bound predicts a particular mixture. It verifies the theorem as stated under
its conditional drift and MGF assumptions; it does not establish those
assumptions for an arbitrary application.

The finite rational sweeps do not by themselves prove the universal theorem.
They accompany the explicit symbolic derivation and detect sign, coefficient,
and normalization errors. The paper's coefficient 5 is sufficient but not
claimed to be sharp.
